# CraftBridge — Video Script (2 minutes)

## Scena 1: Problem (0:00 — 0:30)

**[Fotografija Nataše ili njenih lampi — bliska, topla]**

"We are surrounded by extraordinary people.
Architects. Engineers. Artists.
People who built careers on precision and craft —
and then the world changed.

Nataša is an architect.
When she lost her job, she turned to Tiffany stained glass.
Each lamp — 334 hand-cut pieces. Exceptional work.
The kind that sells for €200 in Amsterdam or Paris.

But Nataša lives in Serbia.
And writing a product listing in German or Japanese?
That's not a skill they teach in architecture school.

Not talent. Not quality. Just words."

---

## Scena 2: Demo — Producer (0:30 — 1:20)

**[Otvori CraftBridge, loguješ se, klikni Add Product]**

"She logs in and adds her lamp.
Instead of typing — she talks. In Serbian."

**[Klikni mikrofon — govoriš:]**
*"Tifani lampa ručne izrade od 334 pažljivo isečena komada stakla. Prečnik abažura 30 cm, visina 50 cm. Svaka lampa je unikat."*

**[Tekst se pojavi — srpski checkbox se čekira automatski]**

"CraftBridge detects Serbian. Fills the field. Zero typing."

**[Čekiraj de, fr, en, ja — klikni Elegant Marketing]**

"She selects Germany, France, Japan, England — one click."

**[Spinner → 4 bloka teksta]**

"Gemma 4 writes professional marketing copy — in all four languages.
Ready to paste into Instagram, Etsy, or an email campaign."

**[Klikni Teen Marketing → pojavi se tekst sa emojijima]**

"Same lamp. Younger audience. Completely different voice.
Both in four languages. In 30 seconds."

---

## Scena 3: Buyer + Impact (1:20 — 2:00)

**[Otvori /shop — klikni language switcher → Deutsch]**

"A buyer in Munich switches to German —
and sees Nataša's lamp described perfectly, for her market."

**[Klikni na proizvod → producer dashboard → leads]**

"She contacts the maker. Nataša gets the lead — in Serbian."

**[Ekran: language switcher sa svim zastavicama]**

"15 languages. 3 marketing tones. Voice input in any language.

Nataša doesn't need to learn German.
She needs to keep making her lamps.

CraftBridge handles the words.

The artisan economy is worth 400 billion dollars a year.
Most of it stays local — because of language.

With Gemma 4, we're changing that.
One maker at a time."

**[Fade out — logo CraftBridge]**

---

## Napomene za snimanje

- **Ton**: Scena 1 — spor, emocionalan, pauze su važne. Scena 2 — brži, praktičan.
- **Fotografije Nataše**: bliske, sa svetlošću kroz staklo — najjači vizualni element
- **Demo**: snimaj na `web-production-0dd34.up.railway.app`
- **Brzina**: ubrzaj 1.5x delove gde čekaš Gemma odgovor
- **Mikrofon**: AirPods, slušalice, ili telefon mikrofon
- **Montaža**: CapCut (besplatno, web/mobilni) ili DaVinci Resolve
