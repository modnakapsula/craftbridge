# CraftBridge — Video Script (3 minutes)

## Scena 1: Problem (0:00 — 0:30)

**[Ekran: fotografija zanatlije koja plete, ili ti sama sa nekim ručnim radom]**

**Govor:**
"Meet Ana. She knits the most beautiful wool sweaters in Niš, Serbia.
Her work is exceptional — the kind that sells for €150 in Berlin or Paris.
But Ana speaks Serbian. Her buyers speak German, French, Japanese.
And writing a product listing in five languages?
That's not a skill she has. So her sweaters stay in Niš."

**[Pauza — ekran zatamni]**

"Three hundred million artisans worldwide face this exact barrier.
Not talent. Not quality. Just language."

---

## Scena 2: Rešenje — Uvod (0:30 — 0:45)

**[Ekran: otvori CraftBridge u browseru — shop stranica]**

**Govor:**
"This is CraftBridge — a marketplace powered by Gemma 4
that removes the language barrier completely.
Let me show you how it works."

---

## Scena 3: Producer flow (0:45 — 1:45)

**[Ekran: klikni "Producer Login" → loguješ se → "Add Product"]**

**Govor:**
"Ana logs in as a producer. She wants to add a new product."

**[Ekran: forma za novi proizvod — klikni mikrofon dugme]**

"Instead of typing, she describes her sweater — by voice, in Serbian."

**[GOVORIŠ NA SRPSKOM u mikrofon — npr:]**
*"Ručno pleteni vuneni džemper od prirodne merino vune u toplim zemljanim tonovima. Svaki džemper je jedinstven i rađen prema narudžbini."*

**[Ekran: tekst se pojavljuje u polju. Checkira sr checkbox automatski]**

"Voice input fills the description automatically.
And — Gemma detects she's speaking Serbian and checks it in the target languages."

**[Ekran: čekiraj de, fr, en, ja — klikni "Elegant Marketing"]**

"Now she selects her target markets — Germany, France, Japan, England —
and clicks Elegant Marketing."

**[Ekran: spinner → pojavljuju se 4 bloka teksta, jedan po jeziku]**

"Gemma 4 writes professional marketing copy — in all four languages —
in her tone, for her audience."

**[Pokaži jedan tekst blok — klikni Copy dugme]**

"Ready to paste into Instagram. Into Etsy. Into an email campaign.
In 30 seconds."

**[Klikni Teen Marketing]**

"Or — for a younger audience — the same product, Gen Z tone."

**[Pojavi se tekst sa emojijima]**

"Same product. Completely different voice. Both in four languages."

---

## Scena 4: Buyer shop (1:45 — 2:30)

**[Ekran: otvori /shop stranicu]**

**Govor:**
"Now let's switch to the buyer side.
A customer in Germany visits CraftBridge."

**[Klikni language switcher → izaberi Deutsch]**

"She switches to German — and the entire shop updates instantly."

**[Ekran: shop sa nemačkim opisima]**

"Ana's sweater — described in perfect German.
The marketing text Gemma wrote for her German audience."

**[Klikni na proizvod → product detail page]**

"She can contact Ana directly through the platform.
Ana gets the lead in her dashboard — in her language."

**[Ekran: producer dashboard → leads sekcija]**

---

## Scena 5: Impact (2:30 — 3:00)

**[Ekran: vrati se na shop — pokaži language switcher sa svim zastavicama]**

**Govor:**
"15 languages. 3 marketing tones. Voice input in any language.
One click from description to global listing."

**[Ekran: logo CraftBridge ili tekst "CraftBridge"]**

"CraftBridge doesn't just translate words.
It translates opportunity.

The artisan economy is worth 400 billion dollars a year.
Most of it stays local — because of language.

With Gemma 4, we're changing that.
One maker at a time."

**[Fade out]**

---

## Praktični saveti za snimanje:

- **Snimaj ekran** sa OBS Studio (besplatno) ili Windows + G (Xbox Game Bar)
- **Mikrofon** — AirPods, slušalice, ili telefon mikrofon
- **Brzina** — možeš da ubrзaš video na 1.5x za delove čekanja (Gemma AI)
- **Jezik** — možeš ceo video na srpskom ako ti je lakše, samo titluj
- **Dužina** — ako prelaziš 3 min, skrati Scenu 4 (buyer flow)

## Alati za montažu:
- **CapCut** (besplatno, mobilni ili web) — lako dodavanje teksta i cut
- **DaVinci Resolve** (besplatno, desktop) — profesionalno
- **iMovie** ako imaš Mac
